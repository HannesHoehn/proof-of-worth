## Why this machine fits the concept

Hand-built and individually tested in Sweden, with a metal gear train instead of plastic gears – the Assistent Original is deliberately built for decades of use, not a low entry price.

## Limitations

The 7-year warranty only applies after registering the machine and only covers the motor base, not attachments. The high purchase price is a real barrier to entry.

## Verdict

A classic example of opportunity-cost thinking: an expensive purchase, but over 20+ years with available spare parts, cheaper in the long run than replacing several lower-cost machines.
