## Why this is deliberately included as a discussion piece

An iPhone shows why "expensive" and "BIFL" aren't the same thing. Apple has visibly improved repairability over recent model years (iFixit score up from 4/10 to a current 7/10), yet core BIFL criteria remain weak: a short warranty period, a lifespan effectively capped by software update cycles, and an annual model-refresh rhythm that drives consumption pressure.

## What speaks in its favor

The high resale value is real and offsets the opportunity cost – a well-maintained iPhone loses value noticeably slower than many Android devices.

## Verdict

No clear yes or no – which is exactly why it belongs on this site: an example of the Proof-of-Worth Score judging things with nuance instead of blanket verdicts.
