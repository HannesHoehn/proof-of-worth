## Why this pot fits the concept

No wear part, no coating that shows scratches after two years – a cast iron dutch oven is essentially as durable as the material itself. Le Creuset's lifetime warranty lasts as long as the original buyer (or someone who received it new as a gift) owns the product.

## Limitations

The warranty doesn't cover commercial use, misuse, or damage from overheating or improper handling. Other Le Creuset product lines (silicone, salt/pepper mills) carry different, shorter warranty periods – the fine print is worth reading.

## Verdict

A high purchase price, but at a realistic 30–40 years of use, one of the best examples of "buy expensive, own cheap."
