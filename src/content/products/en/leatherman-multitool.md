## Why this tool fits the concept

A Leatherman is the textbook example of low time preference applied to an everyday object: no forced model cycle, no built-in wear parts that devalue the rest of the product. The 25-year warranty applies to the original owner and covers material and workmanship defects – not wear from normal use, but that's exactly what the tool is built for.

## Limitations

The warranty doesn't cover corrosion/oxidation or accessories (with a few exceptions). If you use your tool around salt water, it needs proper care.

## Verdict

A clear BIFL fit: a high purchase price, but the cost per year of use approaches zero over a realistic 20+ year lifespan.
