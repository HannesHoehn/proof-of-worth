## Why this is deliberately included as a discussion piece

Crocs are a textbook example of a product that's functionally good (light, comfortable, weatherproof) but scores weakly on nearly every BIFL criterion: not repairable, no meaningful resale value, no extended warranty. Their own "SolesUnited" recycling program is a step toward a circular model, but it doesn't change the short lifespan of the individual product.

## Verdict

A low Proof-of-Worth score – deliberately included as a counter-example, so the scoring logic doesn't just confirm "favorite products" but stays honest where a product genuinely falls short.
