## Why this boot fits the concept

The key difference from disposable footwear is the repair service: a worn-out sole doesn't mean the boot gets thrown away, it means it gets resoled – often several times over the lifetime of the upper.

## Limitations

The statutory/contractual warranty at 2 years isn't unusually long; the real BIFL character only comes from active care and repair, not automatically from warranty claims.

## Verdict

A good example that "Proof of Worth" doesn't just measure warranty length, but real repairability beyond the warranty period.
