## Why this is deliberately included as a discussion piece

On the plus side: many Dyson models are designed with a click-in battery that can be swapped without tools – no soldered-in battery like some competing products. On the minus side: the standard warranty is only 2 years, and real-world lifespan is heavily limited by the high-performance motor and battery cycle count.

## What's missing for a higher score

A longer manufacturer warranty and an official, more affordable spare-parts program for motor and electronics components would meaningfully improve the score.

## Verdict

A middle-of-the-pack result: better than pure throwaway electronics, but no automatic win for the BIFL case either – the cost-per-use calculator is especially worthwhile here compared to cheaper corded vacuums with a longer lifespan.
